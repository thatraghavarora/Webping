Assisted, not autonomous

AI drafts, grounded in your own documents.

Upload your product docs and WebPing drafts replies from them — pricing, policies, the things your team actually types. A human always reviews and hits send; automation can be muted per chat by keyword or sentiment.

  • Grounded in uploaded docs, not hallucinated features.
  • Human-guided — nothing sends from drafts without a person approving it.
  • Interruptible — agents take over any chat in one step.
